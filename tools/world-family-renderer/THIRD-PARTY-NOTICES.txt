The tool references TEdit.Common and TEdit.Terraria at build time and adapts TEdit's PixelMap color-composition/background-zone algorithm in TEditPaletteRenderer.cs. The complete TEdit MS-PL text is included as LICENSE.TEdit.MS-PL.txt.

Terraria is not redistributed by this tool. World generation runs against the user's own local Terraria/gloader installation and private user-derived x64 runtime.
